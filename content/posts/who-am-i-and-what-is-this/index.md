## Who am I?

My name is Rue Asha. Except it isn't, and just like that we have a little
conflict at hand from the very beginning: I am not very keen on sharing personal
things in general, especially when it risks my privacy, but at the same time I
have things I would like to share with the world — or at least with whoever is
remotely interested, for one reason or another.

The way I am solving this is the same way many people who post and speak
publicly online do, to different extents: through a persona. As you might have
guessed, in my case that persona is Rue Asha, and it is still tied to my real
self. Nothing but the name is made up. It is more of a trimmed-down version of
me, so the thoughts, opinions and experiences here are very much my own.

And to be quite honest, there is nothing particularly interesting to know about
me, except that I am a tech nerd who wants to explore the bubble, work on my
skills and, ideally, not end up homeless.

That pretty much answers the question of who I am.

## Where I am coming from

The next reasonable question is what this is, and that is one I can — gladly —
go into more detail on.

All my life I have tinkered with different things in one way or another and
built projects that were rather amateurish in nature. For a while now, though, I
have been studying computer science (which, as we all know, doesn't really teach
you shit) and working part-time as a system administrator. That experience,
alongside the rise of AI, really skyrocketed my knowledge and my skills, and it
gave me a better idea of the industry and of where my interests in the field
lie.

I am still at the beginning of my journey. I have started to realize all the
different things I can do and, more importantly, which things I want to learn —
and which ones I have to.

## Why doing it once is not enough

Learning by doing is well known to be one of the best approaches. But especially
considering a) the sheer amount of information out there and b) the comfort and
productivity AI enables, I realized that just doing things once is definitely
not enough. You have to engage with the creation and learning process very
consciously in order to really grow.

## So, what is this?

Coming back to the question: put plainly, this is a platform for documentation —
of my projects and of my learning process.

To put it more romantically, it is my way of consciously engaging with the new
things I encounter and the things I am learning, by writing about them so that I
am forced to think them through.

I don't really expect anyone to read my stuff, let alone that it has an impact
on someone. I just want to hold myself accountable by publishing these things.
But if someone does happen to read this and gets any sort of value out of it:
thank you, truly, for tagging along on my journey.
