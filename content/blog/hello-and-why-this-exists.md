I keep solving things and then forgetting how. A box falls, I close the terminal, and two
months later I hit the same class of misconfiguration and start from scratch. So: a public
notebook.

## What goes where

**[Writeups](/writeups/)** — boxes, labs and CTF challenges, walked through end to end.
These are the closest thing to polished writing here, but I deliberately leave the dead
ends in. The path that didn't work is usually the part worth remembering.

**[Projects](/projects/)** — documentation for things I build, kept as living pages rather
than dated posts. Each one has a progress log at the bottom so I can see movement.

**[Journal](/blog/)** — this. Short pieces on whatever I learned recently, including the
parts that still don't click.

## Why publish it

Writing for an audience forces a level of precision that private notes never do. If I
can't explain why the exploit worked, I didn't understand it — and that gap is invisible
until I try to write the sentence.

That's the whole thesis. Let's see how it holds up.
