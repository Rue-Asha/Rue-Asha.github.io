## Scope and ethics

Every technique documented here was applied to systems I own or was explicitly authorised
to test: retired HackTheBox machines, TryHackMe rooms, CTF infrastructure, and my own
homelab. Nothing on this site is guidance for attacking systems you don't have permission
to touch.
