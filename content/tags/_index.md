---
title: Tags
---

Tags are the one axis that cuts across everything here. Sections tell you *what kind* of
page you're looking at — a writeup, a project, a journal entry — and folders tell you which
platform a box came from. Neither can answer "everything I've touched involving Kerberos,"
which spans all three. That's what these are for.

Counts show how many pages carry each tag. Anything in **Unfiled** is a tag used in content
but missing from the vocabulary in `data/tags.yaml` — usually a typo or a near-synonym that
needs merging.
