| R-01 | Major | `construction/u1-publishable-site-shell/functional-design/entities.md` > entity `Post`, attribute `date` (min/max) vs. `construction/u1-publishable-site-shell/functional-design/rules.md` > `BR2.3` | `entities.md` declares `Post.date` bounds — `min: "1970-01-01"`, `max: the build's own date, plus 366 days` — as part of the attribute's constraint. `functional-spec.md` (§ intro) states both YAML blocks are independently authoritative: `entities.md` for "types, constraints, and bounds" and `rules.md` for "decision logic" and violation behaviour. But `BR2.3`, the rule that owns `Post.date` validation, checks only that the value strictly matches `YYYY-MM-DD` as a real calendar date — its `logic` and `on_violation` text say nothing about a lower or upper bound, and no other rule in `rules.md` enforces one. A developer building from `rules.md` (the enforcement-logic source of truth) would never reject a 1969 date or a date five years in the future; a developer building from `entities.md` would reject both. The two co-authoritative files disagree on whether this bound exists, unlike the equivalent `Project.year` bound, which `entities.md` states and `BR3.2` enforces identically (`IF year is not an integer, or is below 1970, or is above the build's own year plus one THEN field error`) — showing the pattern is otherwise applied correctly and this is a specific omission, not a stated design choice. | Either add the min/max check and its violation message to `BR2.3` in `rules.md` (mirroring `BR3.2`'s pattern for `Project.year`), or remove the bound from `entities.md` and record why no bound applies to `Post.date` (e.g., as a stated assumption). The two files must agree on whether the constraint is enforced. | New |

### Summary

The artifact set is unusually disciplined — every rule states its violation behaviour, the component dependency graph is genuinely acyclic, and the traceability file's forward and reverse coverage checks out against the story map's FR assignment for U1. The two Major findings are narrow, concrete contradictions between co-authoritative files (a date bound stated in `entities.md` with no matching enforcement in `rules.md`, and a validation step excluded from the fail-loudly aggregation list it is annotated against) rather than structural flaws in the design; both are fixable by tightening one table each. Neither blocks a developer from building the bulk of this unit, but both should be closed before Code Generation locks in the ambiguity. The two Minor findings are documentation-completeness gaps consistent with this document's own stated practice of flagging additions and completions explicitly.
