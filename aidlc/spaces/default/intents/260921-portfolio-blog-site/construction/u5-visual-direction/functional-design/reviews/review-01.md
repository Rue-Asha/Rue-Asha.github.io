### Summary

The unusual zero-FR/five-NFR traceability shape, the `reverse` entry, and every cross-unit rule citation (BR5.2, BR5.10) check out against the upstream artifacts and mechanical tooling. The design fails on two things a developer would actually trip over: BR11.1's 44px-padding rule for standalone links, applied literally to the Projects-row outbound link that `frontend-components.md` itself names as subject to it, numerically conflicts with the 16px anti-mis-hit clear-space the interaction spec deliberately sized for that exact element (R-01, Critical); and the traceability table's claim that NFR6 is covered by BR11.1 does not match BR11.1's own stated source or content, leaving NFR6's real layout-contraction requirements without a rule that actually backs them (R-02, Major). One Critical finding is sufficient to block readiness on its own.
