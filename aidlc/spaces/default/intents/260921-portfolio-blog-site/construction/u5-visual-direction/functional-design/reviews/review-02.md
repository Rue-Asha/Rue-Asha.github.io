### Summary

Every finding from iteration 1 is genuinely resolved: the BR11.1/clear-space arithmetic works out and the hit-area-based reconciliation is a sound fix rather than an assertion (R-01); BR11.6 now gives NFR6 real rule backing with a consistent `source:` field and traceability target (R-02); the enforcement-table rewrite accurately distinguishes browser-runtime enforcement from the pre-push check set (R-03); and the platform-specific wording flagged in `frontend-components.md` is gone (R-04). The revision itself introduces one new Critical problem: BR11.6, added to cover NFR6, quotes NFR6's "list row keeps ONE hit target" language without qualification, which directly contradicts BR11.1 and `frontend-components.md`'s own "U3 rows" entry — both of which require the Projects-row outbound link to be a second, independent 44px target below the breakpoint. A developer cannot satisfy both rules as written for that element (R-05). A second, Major gap was found by applying the same coverage-semantics check the builder used to fix R-02 to NFR1: its traceability row claims full coverage via two rules that address only one of its four conditions, leaving tab-order preservation and focus-trap avoidance backed by an assumption and a manual step rather than a rule (R-06). One Critical finding is sufficient to block readiness.
