### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound — no broken references, no asymmetry, no cycles, full FR traceability, and every ADR carries all four required sections — and the six fold-backs the revision pass did perform (project ordering, asset attributes, library names, the two `SiteBuilder` edges, `FieldError` naming) were checked against `src/` and are each accurate. However, the pass's own stated aim was to bring the catalogue into full agreement with the built system, and two further real divergences survived it: `Project` is missing its `body` attribute (present in the code, and flagged in the code's own comments as a known gap versus the design), and an entire cross-component validation behaviour (BR8.2's fenced-code-language check, spanning `MarkupRenderer` and `SiteBuilder`) is undocumented anywhere in the catalogue. Both are readable directly from `src/` with the same kind of inspection [Q13] already applied to find the two missing dependency edges, so they read as incomplete execution of an otherwise sound reconciliation method rather than a flaw in the method itself.
