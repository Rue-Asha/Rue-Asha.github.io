| R-02 | Major | `wireframes.md` > Project (in-depth page) and > Assumptions & Open Questions; `rough-mockups-questions.md` Q6; cf. `scope-definition/scope-document.md` "Projects" row and `scope-definition/intent-backlog.md` PU-3 | The approved MVP scope and backlog define "Projects" as one capability — "a section presenting what you have built" (PU-3) — with no per-project detail page named anywhere upstream. The Q6 free-text answer added a second page type (an individual, per-project in-depth write-up page with its own metadata rail, its own accessibility note, its own screen-state row, and its own IA node) — a materially larger surface than the approved scope enumerates. The stage protocol calls for "contradiction analysis between UX expectations and scope constraints" (Step 3) precisely for this situation, but no such analysis is visible anywhere in the artifacts; the expansion is folded straight into the frames and then flagged only as a downstream assumption ("this reading has not been separately confirmed against the approved boundary"). Labeling a scope expansion as an open assumption after the fact is not the same as running the contradiction check the stage mandates before committing the frames. Recommend routing the per-project detail page back through scope-definition (or an explicit backlog amendment) for confirmation before Refined Mockups builds on top of it, rather than carrying it forward as an unresolved assumption. |
| R-03 | Minor | `wireframes.md` accessibility notes for Home, Post, Projects, and Project (`<time datetime>`, `<dl>`, `<pre><code>`) | The stage asks for a one-line accessibility note naming heading level, landmark region, and keyboard entry point — the phase rule (`ideation.md`) separately forbids implementation details (architecture, tech stack, code) in ideation artifacts. These notes go further, naming specific HTML elements (`<time datetime>`, `<dl>`, `<pre><code>`) rather than describing the accessibility semantics in prose. This is a small step past "boxes and labels only" into markup-level implementation choice, even though the underlying intent (announce label/value pairs, keep dates machine-readable) is sound. Consider restating these as behavior ("dates are announced unambiguously to assistive technology," "metadata is announced as label/value pairs") and leaving the concrete element choice to Refined Mockups or Construction. |

### Summary

The wireframes are well-grounded — nearly every claim carries an inline source tag, the five-state rule is honestly applied (the "not applicable" loading state is labeled and reasoned rather than dodged), and the mermaid flow diagram in `user-flow.md` is syntactically valid with a complete text fallback. The two Major findings are worth the human's attention before approving: an internal contradiction about whether About gets its own frame, and a real scope-boundary question — the Q6-driven per-project detail page is a legitimate elaboration in spirit but was never run through the contradiction check the stage itself calls for, and it visibly grows the surface the approved scope document described.
