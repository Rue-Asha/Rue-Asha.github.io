# Intent Statement — Personal Portfolio & Blog Site

## Sources

- [desc] Initial description: "I want to build a Github.io Blog site to present my work and make blog posts about things I currently learning or I find to be interesting. For UX/UI I want you to use Mobbin"
- [scope] Workflow-selected scope: `feature`.

---

## Problem Statement

There is work worth showing and nowhere good to point people at. [Q4]

The intent is a GitHub Pages site that both presents that work and carries blog
posts about things currently being learned or found interesting. [desc] The two
halves are weighted equally: the site is a portfolio and a blog side by side,
rather than one serving the other. [Q1]

## Target Customer

Two reader groups matter equally: recruiters and hiring managers, and other
developers. [Q2]

Neither group is served by the current situation, because no such site exists
yet — the absence of a place to point people at is the stated problem. [Q4]
The specific difficulty each group experiences today has not been stated; see
`## Assumptions & Open Questions`.

## Success Metrics

The success bar is qualitative by explicit decision: the site exists and its
owner is satisfied with it. [Q9]

Someone reaching out because of something they read on the site is a welcome
outcome but explicitly not a target. [Q9] No visitor counts, page views, or
other analytics figures are being treated as success signals. [Q3]

This is a deliberate choice rather than an omission, and it means this
initiative carries no numeric outcome target; that consequence is recorded
under `## Assumptions & Open Questions`.

## Initiative Trigger

Accumulated work with no good place to present it. [Q4] There is no external
deadline, client commitment, or event driving the timing — the trigger is the
gap itself. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

| Signal | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow-selected scope | `feature` | [scope] (workflow-selected) |
| User-confirmed product boundary | The whole site, built end to end | [Q8] |

The workflow-selected scope and the user-confirmed boundary agree: the
initiative covers a complete site rather than an addition to something that
already exists. [Q8]

## Stated Direction

Mobbin is named in the initial description as the source to use for UX/UI.
[desc] How that direction is applied is decided at the mockup and design
stages, not here.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- [assumption] The specific difficulty each reader group faces today is
  unstated. The audiences are confirmed [Q2], but what recruiters or developers
  currently fail to find has not been established and should be confirmed before
  requirements fix the site's navigation and per-post context.
- [assumption] With no measurable success criterion by explicit choice [Q9],
  this initiative has no quantitative outcome the later phases can verify
  against. Any measurable target introduced downstream (page speed,
  accessibility level, publishing cadence) would be a new decision, not a
  restatement of this one.
- [assumption] Content volume at launch — how many projects and how many posts
  the site needs to hold on day one — has not been stated and is not derivable
  from any confirmed answer.
